Saltline Cover Studio turns your own photos and a caption into multi-panel GTA-style cover art: pick a layout, and every panel of your photos is auto-graded, mapped through a Vice City sunset or neon gradient ramp, inked into poster bands, screened with halftone dots and framed — then exported as a PNG in 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, 1920 × 1080 or 1080 × 1920. Seven layouts (mosaic grid, hero sleeve, cover story, tilted snapshots, duo, reel strips, full bleed), six colour grades, a subject-aware smart crop that finds the subject in each photo and frames it for the panel's shape, per-panel drag and zoom, undo/redo, and a one-click Auto Cover. Fully offline: photos are read on your own machine and never uploaded. Lettering uses the Anton and Pacifico typefaces under the SIL Open Font License (see app/fonts/).

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Or serve the `app/` folder with any static file server — `app/index.html` is the whole app.
